SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lumen Ridge Analytics Ltd (the "Supplier") and Bramblewood Growers Cooperative (the "Customer").

1. Definitions
"Services" means the soil and yield analysis services the Supplier provides to the Customer.
"Initial Term" means the twelve months starting on the day both parties sign this Agreement.
"Affiliate" means an entity that controls, or is controlled by, a party.

2. Term
This Agreement has an initial term of twelve months.
This Agreement renews for one year unless either party objects in writing.

3. Services
The Supplier shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care.

4. Fees
4.1. The Customer shall pay each invoice within 30 days.
4.2. Fees are non-refundable, except as provided in Section 8.4.
4.3. The Supplier may raise its fees once a year, provided that it gives the Customer 60 days' notice.

5. Liability
5.1. Subject to Section 5.2, the Supplier is liable for all losses its breach causes.
5.2. Notwithstanding Section 5.1, the Supplier is not liable for indirect losses.

6. Assignment
The Supplier may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate.

7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario.

8. Termination
8.1. Subject to Section 8.3, either party may terminate this Agreement on 60 days' written notice.
8.2. The Supplier may terminate this Agreement at once if an invoice stays unpaid for 45 days.
8.3. Notwithstanding Section 8.1, neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience during the Initial Term.
8.4. The Supplier shall refund prepaid fees if it terminates this Agreement under Section 8.1.
